LEAGUE FORMAT

Rules

01

16 Teams

Sixteen owners compete in one league table with no divisions.

02

DraftKings Lineups

Each owner enters a weekly DFS lineup on DraftKings. That lineup's final DK score becomes the team's score for the week.

03

Head-to-Head

Eight matchups are played each week. The higher DraftKings score earns the win.

04

15-Week Regular Season

Weeks 1–15. Every team plays every other team exactly once.

05

Tiebreaker

Standings are sorted by overall record, then Total DK Points For.

06

Top 4 Playoffs

Week 16: #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3. Week 17: championship between the semifinal winners.
