LEAGUE FORMAT

Rules

01

DraftKings Lineups

Each owner submits one lineup in the league-designated DraftKings NFL Classic contest each week. The lineup's final DK score becomes the team's score for that week.

02

Head-to-Head

Eight matchups are played each week. The team with the higher DraftKings score earns the win.

03

Regular Season

Weeks 1–15. Every team plays every other team exactly once.

04

Standings & Tiebreakers

Standings are determined by winning percentage. Ties are first broken by Total DK Points For. If still tied, the next tiebreaker is head-to-head result.

05

Top 4 Playoffs

The top four teams qualify. Week 16: #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3. Week 17: championship between the semifinal winners.

IMPORTANT
06

Lineup Submission Deadline

Every owner must join the league-designated DraftKings contest before the first game of that contest kicks off. Miss the deadline and you receive 0 DK points and a loss for that week's matchup. No exceptions.

ADDITIONAL RULES
Regular-Season TiesAn exact DK score tie is recorded as a tie. Standings use winning percentage.
Playoff TiesIf a playoff matchup ends in an exact tie, the higher playoff seed advances or wins the championship.
Total Points TieIf two or more owners finish tied for the most Total DK Points For after Week 17, the $500 prize is split evenly.
Official ScoringThe final score officially recorded by DraftKings is the score used by Big Bucks Fantasy League.
Weeks 16–17All 16 owners should continue entering the weekly contest. Those scores count toward the season-long Total Points prize.
